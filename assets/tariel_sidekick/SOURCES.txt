The paladin and the hooded are Synty's Starter_01 and Starter_03 recipes; the
sworn and the iron knight are mixes of the same parts (the knight's kit, human
heads, hair and beards) in Starter_01's and Starter_03's colour maps with other
body shapes. Each look's parts are joined into one mesh on Sidekick's own
88-bone skeleton; its rest turned onto tariel_rig's limb directions and the
hands closed, two bones (weapon_r, shield_l) and Tariel's sword and shields
added by vepxis-art/tools/sk_build.py.
