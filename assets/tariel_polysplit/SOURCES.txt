BasicHeroes/M_Swordsman.fbx as Polysplit dressed him (gambeson, skull cap over
a mail coif, scabbard), the male base head, the pack's pre-coloured textures
(medievalTexture_bodyColor1, medievalTexture_objectColor1). The parts are
joined into one mesh on Polysplit's own 98-bone skeleton; its rest turned onto
tariel_rig's limb directions and the hands closed, two bones (weapon_r,
shield_l) and Tariel's shields added, and the pack's own sword put where
Tariel's sword is, by vepxis-art/tools/ps_build.py.
