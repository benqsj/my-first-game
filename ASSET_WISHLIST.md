# Asset wishlist

Asset packs chosen or considered for Vepxis (decided 2026-10-02). The art
direction is **stylized low poly**, so every pack should match the
Polysplit Heroes look. Packs come through Unity Hub: Package Manager, then
My Assets, then Download. They are unpacked without Unity, as was done for
Blink and Sidekick.

## Buying now
- **Low-Poly Medieval Fantasy Heroes – Basic Pack** (Polysplit Games), $24.99
  https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/characters/humanoids/fantasy/low-poly-medieval-fantasy-heroes-basic-pack-288957
  - Modular: 2 base bodies (male and female), 9 class outfits, hair, beards, faces, weapons.
  - About 3k tris per character. One 2048 RGB colour-mask texture plus 22 pre-coloured PNGs.
  - Humanoid rig with no animations. It will follow `tariel_rig` through `FigureFollower`, as Sidekick does.

## Animations: test the free versions, then buy one
- **Quaternius UAL 2**: the free Standard version is already in `assets/anim/ual2.glb`.
  The paid version is $14.99 and up, with 110+ clips (bow, parkour, sword combos) on the same rig.
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library-2
- **Kevin Iglesias – Human Melee Animations**: free version first, to test.
  It needs a one-time retarget, which carries over to the paid pack.
  - Free: https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/animations/human-melee-animations-free-165785
  - Paid: $23 list price, $11.50 on the Unity Store sale (Oct 2026). It has 284 clips: sword, shield, two-handed, polearm, block, parry, dodge.
    https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/animations/human-melee-animations-151650
    https://kevdev.itch.io/human-melee-animations

## Later
- **NPCs: Low-Poly Medieval Fantasy – NPC Pack** (Polysplit Games), $10. Same style as Heroes.
  https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/characters/low-poly-medieval-fantasy-npc-pack-317450
- **Monsters: Low-Poly Medieval Fantasy – Biped Creatures Pack** (Polysplit Games), $14.99.
  https://assetstore-fallback.unity.com/publishers/101065
- **Buildings**: look at the free CC0 low-poly kits from Quaternius and KayKit first.
  BlenderKit Full ($9.90/month, https://www.blendkit.com/plans/pricing/) only if those are not enough.
  Check its licence before subscribing (what happens to downloads after cancelling), and keep only low-poly assets.
